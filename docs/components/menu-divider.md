# MenuDivider

MenuDivider is the line between groups of items in a menu.

## When to use

Use it between groups of [MenuItem](menu-item.md), and before the one
destructive item at the end of a menu.

## Props

MenuDivider has no props.

## Contract

It is one line of the line colour with no space above or below: the
hover surface of the items meets it, and the padding of the items is the
distance. Its role is `separator`.
