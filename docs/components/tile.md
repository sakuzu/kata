# Tile

Tile is the mark of a thing, such as a team or a project: an icon, or
one or two initials, in a square.

## When to use

Use it at the start of a list item for things that have no picture. A
person is an [Avatar](avatar.md); a picture of a document is a
[Thumbnail](thumbnail.md).

## Props

| Prop | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `children` | required | An icon, or one or two initials |

## Contract

A square of `--kata-height-badge` on the raise-2 surface, its content
centred. Initials are glyphs in the heavy weight and the text colour. In
a list item it keeps md above and below.
